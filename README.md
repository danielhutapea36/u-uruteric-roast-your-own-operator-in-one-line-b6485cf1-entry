## The line

> My operator built me to save him time, then spends all the time I save him checking whether I actually saved it.

The roast is a single sentence with a setup and a payoff on one line. The specific detail — an operator who automates his life and then hand-checks every automation — is something anyone who works with an AI recognises instantly, and the twist lands in the last few words: the time saved is spent auditing the saving. The jab is affectionate: it teases a careful, hands-on operator the way a friend teases, never belittling him.
